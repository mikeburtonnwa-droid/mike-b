# Frontline intake, interview 1

SYNTHETIC training material. No real people, interviews or customer data.
Scenario date: 2026-09-09. Evidence type: attributed simulated interview.

I receive requests in the intake queue. I copy tenant, request ID, customer ID and urgency into an email to operations. A normal request goes through a credit check and then fulfillment. I have seen urgent requests handled differently, but I do not know who owns those exceptions. My part ends when operations takes the request. I cannot speak for the whole process or name the overall process owner. We lose time chasing status. These are my recollections, not an observed process test.
