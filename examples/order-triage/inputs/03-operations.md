# Operations lead and workflow owner, interview 3

SYNTHETIC training material. No real people, interviews or customer data.
Scenario date: 2026-09-09. Evidence type: attributed simulated interview.

I own the bounded workflow from intake acceptance to recorded routing disposition. Current policy in this synthetic scenario, effective 2026-09-01: frontline sends the request to operations; operations triages urgency. Urgent requests go to the service desk for manual review and stop there with a disposition. Standard requests go to finance for the latest credit decision. Approved requests go to fulfillment; denied or missing decisions stay in finance review. Finance does not own urgent exceptions. The boundary ends at the routing disposition, before shipment or financial authorization. I confirmed this by walking all four fixture requests with the participants. The service desk operates across the internal/support boundary; fulfillment is a separate internal system.
