# Process discovery

Inputs: scoped problem, stakeholder material, existing context and known variants. Read process/coverage definitions in [records](../../docs/RECORDS.md) and anthropology/process methods in [professional references](../../references/professional-methods.md).

Capture original accounts and exact locators. Separate reported practice, observed work, policy and interpretation. Notice incentives, workarounds, invisible coordination and unrepresented voices. Respect participants' sharing constraints; do not infer permission to contact anyone.

Trace a concrete case from trigger to outcome. Record actor, system, boundary, input, output and evidence for every step. Label every branch. Identify payload and receiving actor where owner, system or boundary changes. Seek failed, delayed, rejected and unusual cases where relevant. Preserve variant-specific accounts instead of averaging them into one happy path.

Declare stakeholder, variant and exception coverage with evidence and mapped steps, or explicit gaps with owner/next action. Keep `unknown` visible. A connected graph does not prove complete discovery. Cross-check originals and seek appropriate stakeholder confirmation within existing permissions.

Outputs: source/claim IDs, current process nodes/edges, coverage records, derived current map and gaps. Run `check process --view current`; explain failures in business terms. Future improvements belong in proposed nodes with baseline links.

Handoff: inspected inputs, map revision, coverage limits, conflicts, highest-value next interview/observation and checkpoint. After one interview, keep the map provisional and record absent owner/exception evidence. Do not manufacture branches to pass a check.
